My Location

Used for the blue dot on the map and for weather alerts.

Location source
The system location is often a rough guess from your IP address. Set your real spot below for accuracy.

Addresses are looked up with the US Census geocoder (or OpenStreetMap); towns with Open-Meteo. Only what you type is sent, and only when you search.

Tip: zoom the map to your house, then press Use Map Center — or click a saved place's marker and choose 📍 My location.
